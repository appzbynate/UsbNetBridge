# UsbNetBridge Promotional Video Script (30 Seconds)

**Goal:** Show how magically simple it is to use the app in under 30 seconds.
**What you need:** Your phone, a PC, a USB device (like a webcam or thumb drive), and screen recording software (or just film the screens with another camera).

### The Shot List

**[0:00 - 0:05] The Setup**
* **Visual:** Close-up of your hands plugging the USB device (e.g. a thumb drive) into the bottom of the Android phone. 
* **Text Overlay (Optional):** "Plug in any USB device."

**[0:05 - 0:10] The Android App**
* **Visual:** Screen recording of the Android app. The USB device appears in the list. You tap the bright cyan "Start" button.
* **Text Overlay:** "Tap Start to broadcast over Wi-Fi."

**[0:10 - 0:15] The Windows App**
* **Visual:** Screen recording of the Windows PC Desktop. The UsbNetBridge client is open. The phone instantly appears in the "USB hosts" list automatically.
* **Text Overlay:** "Auto-discovers on your network."

**[0:15 - 0:25] The Magic Connection**
* **Visual:** The mouse clicks "Connect" on the Windows app. A Windows notification pops up saying "USB Drive Connected" (or the file explorer instantly opens showing the drive's contents).
* **Text Overlay:** "Use it on your PC wirelessly!"

**[0:25 - 0:30] Outro**
* **Visual:** The UsbNetBridge logo fades in.
* **Text Overlay:** "UsbNetBridge. Download now on Google Play."
